# Research and surveys

## Research questions

- Which wheat growth stages and visible symptoms can phone photos capture reliably?
- When does a 15–25 day revisit help, and which field conditions call for a shorter interval?
- Which capture instructions improve comparability across angle, lighting, and growth stage?
- Which forecast variables warrant inspection, and how should uncertainty be communicated?

Sources to investigate include local agricultural extension services, CIMMYT wheat resources, FAO publications, and peer-reviewed wheat phenology and crop monitoring studies. Check region, cultivar, growth stage, and study conditions before applying guidance.

## Proposed farmer survey

1. How do you currently track changes in a wheat field across visits?
2. How often do you inspect it, and what prompts an extra inspection?
3. What makes it difficult to take or compare field photos?
4. Which observations or weather cautions would help you decide where to look next?
5. Which language and format would you prefer for explanations?
6. What would make you comfortable sharing field photos or approximate location?

## Evidence collection

Collect consent before recording responses or using photos. Record collection dates, participant counts, sampling limits, and source references. For photo pairs, retain capture dates and relevant context with participant permission. Summarize real responses only after collection and label hypothetical examples or fixtures wherever used.
