# Farmer survey

## Introduction

We are exploring how growers and field scouts track crop changes and decide when to inspect a field. This voluntary survey takes about five minutes. You can skip any question or stop at any time. Please leave out names, contact details, and precise field locations. We will summarize answers together. We will ask separately before using any quotation or photograph.

## Questionnaire

1. What crops do you monitor, and what is your role in those checks?
2. How often do you inspect a field during the growing season?
3. How do you record changes between visits, if at all?
4. Which signs of crop stress or disease are hardest to recognize?
5. When you need advice, where do you turn and how easy is it to get help?
6. Which weather events most often change your inspection plans?
7. How often can you use a smartphone and internet connection in the field?
8. Which language and format would you choose for a field observation summary?
9. When would comparing photos of the same field 15–25 days apart be useful?
10. What would make you trust, question, or ignore a recommendation from an AI tool?

## Analysis template

Record recruitment method, fieldwork dates, number invited when known, number responding, and valid responses per question. For multiple-choice summaries report counts and denominators. Code open answers for recurring themes and keep contradictory feedback visible. Store consent separately from responses and avoid quoting identifiable details.
