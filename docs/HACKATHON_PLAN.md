# Hackathon plan

Validate the feasibility of taking comparable wheat photos across visits, obtaining permission to use them, and getting structured Gemini observations within the demo time. Track what was tested and what remains unverified.

## MVP and demo

1. Open a wheat field with a dated baseline observation.
2. Scan a recent image taken 15–25 days later and show the visible observations.
3. Compare the two photos side by side and explain a visible change and a limitation.
4. Use a labeled recording of a verified run if live connectivity fails. Label any illustrative fixtures.

The MVP is one field, two dated observations, and one comparison. Add weather, reminders, or bilingual explanations only after the core flow is stable.

## Presentation and judge Q&A

Present the problem, show the two-date workflow, explain why the field history matters, and close with the evidence collected and scope limits. Prepare answers on poor photo quality, missing baselines, user privacy, the wheat-first scope, and what information would require an agronomist.

## Submission checklist

- Confirm submission requirements and deadline.
- Rehearse the demo and prepare the fallback recording.
- Verify setup instructions and remove credentials from the submission.
- Confirm photo permissions, citations, and labels for demo fixtures.
- Submit the required repository, presentation, and demo links.
