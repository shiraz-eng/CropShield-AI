# Research evidence

| Evidence | Relevance to CropShield |
| --- | --- |
| CIMMYT, wheat evidence brief. [CIMMYT repository](https://repository.cimmyt.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/2f390f5f-7ec4-4197-92e0-d35921fee7a4/content) | Wheat is an important food crop, including for resource-constrained consumers. The project starts with wheat to limit the scope of capture guidance and evaluation. |
| Asseng et al., 2015. [Rising temperatures reduce global wheat production](https://doi.org/10.1038/nclimate2470) | The multi-model study found a negative temperature effect on global wheat production. This supports investigating field weather risk. Local severity thresholds still need agronomic validation. |
| Mohanty, Hughes and Salathé, 2016. [Image-based plant disease detection](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpls.2016.01419) | The study demonstrated image classification and reported substantially poorer performance on images captured under different conditions. Field photos need representative validation and explicit uncertainty. |
| Mrisho et al., 2020. [Field and on-screen evaluation of PlantVillage Nuru](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpls.2020.590889) | This smartphone tool was evaluated on cassava foliar symptoms. Its field protocol illustrates the importance of capturing multiple views. Its findings do not establish wheat performance. |
| David et al., 2021. [GWHD dataset and release](https://zenodo.org/records/5092309) | Includes wheat-head bounding boxes and is a candidate source for wheat-positive images. Head annotations do not establish growth-stage, symptom, or same-plot longitudinal labels. |
| Open-Meteo. [Forecast API documentation](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs) | Provides a candidate source for forecast values, times and units. App cautions would be derived from forecast inputs and must be clearly distinguished from official alerts. |

## Assumptions to test

- Farmers can consistently repeat a photograph from a useful location and angle.
- A 15–25 day interval helps some wheat monitoring decisions. This is a product default, not a validated agronomic rule.
- Ownership-aware photo history and English or Urdu summaries fit current inspection practices.

The farmer questionnaire is ready for collection. CropShield has no survey findings, quantified yield impact, or measured field accuracy.
