# Project overview

Farmers often rely on memory or scattered photos to judge how a field has changed. CropShield AI aims to give growers and field scouts a dated record they can review before deciding where to inspect next. Initial support is limited to wheat.

- **Scan and interpret.** Crop Scanner records visible observations. Crop Doctor explains symptoms and possible concerns.
- **Compare the same field over time.** Crop Memory Engine pairs observations 15–25 days apart and explains visible changes alongside the source images.
- **Plan inspections.** Weather Guardian provides forecast-based cautions, and Smart Reminders schedule follow-ups.
- **Maintain and explain history.** Field Management keeps dated records, Progress Reports summarize change, and the Farmer Assistant explains observations in English and Urdu.

Expected benefits include easier follow-up, clearer field histories, and more consistent communication with advisers.

Lighting, camera angle, crop stage, and image quality can distort comparisons. Some problems cannot be assessed visually. Forecasts have local uncertainty, and suspected crop problems may require an in-person assessment.
